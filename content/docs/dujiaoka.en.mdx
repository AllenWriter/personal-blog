Do you want to be your own boss? Do you want financial freedom? Who doesn't?

So have you decided what to sell? Your time? Your skills? Or your soul?

Thanks to the internet, the bar for starting a business has become very low. You can get rich just by selling a string of numbers. Activation codes, game top-up cards, and recharge cards you see all over web pages are just strings of digits, yet they can sell for tens, hundreds, or even thousands of yuan.

Opening a shop like this doesn't require tens of thousands of yuan in investment. You just need a server, and you can set up an online convenience store with the [Dujiaoka](https://github.com/assimon/dujiaoka/wiki/2.x_bt_install) project.

## What is Dujiaoka?

Simply put, Dujiaoka is a small dynamic website framework. It includes a complete storefront frontend, a database backend, payment interfaces, a card-delivery interface, and an admin management system. Among card-selling projects, it's a pretty good-looking Web project that a fair number of people use. In short, if you want to open an online card-selling shop, Dujiaoka will definitely be a top recommendation in search engines.

## What is the card-selling model?

The card-selling model is also jokingly called the "internet convenience store" project. On a website, you obtain some card codes through various methods, and then sell those codes to your customers through payment interfaces. You might ask, what kind of password makes money like that? You could write some guides and then set a password to restrict reading, and then sell that password in your little store. Isn't that the simplest form of content monetization?

Or, for example, you could use some clever tricks to legally obtain game cards, such as QQ memberships, WeChat memberships, game top-up cards, game activation codes, software activation codes, and even recharge cards, such as phone top-up cards, data top-up cards, and game recharge cards. Then you sell those card codes to your customers through payment interfaces. That's the card-selling model.

And [Dujiaoka](https://github.com/assimon/dujiaoka/wiki/2.x_bt_install) is a pretty good open-source card-selling website program.
