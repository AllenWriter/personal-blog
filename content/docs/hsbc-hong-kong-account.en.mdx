### Branch Opening Hours

Hong Kong banks are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Hong Kong public holidays include: January 1 (New Year's Day), the first and second days of Lunar New Year, Ching Ming Festival, Labour Day (May 1), Tuen Ng Festival (Dragon Boat Festival), HKSAR Establishment Day (July 1), Mid-Autumn Festival, National Day (October 1), Chung Yeung Festival, and Christmas.

### Account Costs

No account management fee, no deposit requirement. Same-bank transfers within Hong Kong are free of charge. Transfers abroad cost HKD 50 per transaction for mainland China. Transfers to a same-name HSBC account in mainland China are free.

## ZA Bank Guide

ZA Bank is an internet bank. You don't need to go to a branch in person. Just download the app, set your phone's location to Hong Kong, and continue the account application. Upload your ID card and Hong Kong and Macau Travel Permit photos, verify your savings card, and then you can apply to open an account. Wait for a call from ZA, answer a few questions, and after manual review, the account opening is complete.
