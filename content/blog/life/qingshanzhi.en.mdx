## Introduction

Qingshanzhi is an English-language show about an unfinished love between a human and a ghost. It premiered in the middle of 2017 at Chengxi Small Auditorium. The story is carefully constructed, and everyone who watched it was moved to tears. The show is split into two parts, and I hope it can still move you today.
