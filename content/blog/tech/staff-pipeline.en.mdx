## Rehearsal

I wrote full character profiles for the digital staff: who takes the job, who transcribes, who drafts the outline, who writes the finished text, and who prepares the paste-ready copy. The roles were complete, and the handoff diagram looked good. Only once it actually ran did two things become clear. Transcription has two sets of tools, and publishing has no API, so the pipeline can only produce a copy you paste in by hand. The finished audio follows a separate path. With those two lines unconnected, Gangqun stalls at "looking like it's working."

## A Character Profile Is Not a Product

Digital staff are very easy to turn into role-play. The more complete the persona, the more it looks already delivered. A character profile is not a product. If you can't say clearly where the files go, what counts as done, and where the next step picks up, the pipeline is just a rehearsal.

## The Shortest Path

My standard now is pretty plain. Can a piece go from raw material to a manuscript you can take away, or to audio that's ready to air? If it can't get there, then however busy Gangqun gets, it's still a rehearsal. So first open the shortest path: a link comes in, a manuscript goes out. Other capabilities can come later. Pile on too many abilities and the entrance gets muddy again. It's the same disease as the directory problem.
